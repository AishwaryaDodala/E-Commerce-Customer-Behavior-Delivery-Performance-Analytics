# E-Commerce-Customer-Behavior-Delivery-Performance-Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview


The **E-Commerce Customer Behavior & Delivery Performance Analytics** project focuses on analyzing transactional e-commerce data using **Microsoft Excel** to understand sales performance, customer behavior, promotional discounts, product performance, and delivery efficiency.

The project uses **500 order records** containing customer, product, pricing, coupon, order date, and delivery information. The data was cleaned and transformed to create meaningful analytical fields such as **Full Name, Gross Sales, Discount Amount, Delivery Charge, Net Revenue, Delivery Duration, and Delivery Status**.

The analysis was used to identify **high-value customers, top-performing products, leading cities, promotional discount patterns, and delivery performance**. A key finding was that **42% of orders were delivered late**, highlighting a potential fulfillment and customer satisfaction issue.

An interactive **Excel dashboard** was developed to present the key KPIs and insights in a clear and business-friendly format. The project demonstrates how raw e-commerce data can be transformed into **actionable insights that support data-driven business decisions**.


## 📌 Business Objective


The primary objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce transactional data and generate actionable business insights using **Microsoft Excel**.

The analysis focuses on:

* Identifying **high-value customers and top-performing products**.
* Evaluating the impact of **discounts and promotional coupons** on revenue.
* Analyzing **city-wise sales and order performance**.
* Measuring **delivery performance** and identifying late-delivery patterns.
* Identifying potential **fulfillment and operational bottlenecks**.
* Supporting better decisions around **sales, marketing, and supply-chain operations**.
* Improving **customer satisfaction and overall business performance** through data-driven insights.

## 🧩 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)


* **Total Revenue** – Total revenue generated from all orders.
* **Total Orders** – Total number of orders placed.
* **Total Customers** – Number of unique customers who placed orders.
* **Total Quantity Sold** – Total number of units sold.
* **Average Order Value (AOV)** – Average revenue generated per order.
* **Average Delivery Days** – Average time taken to deliver orders.
* **Total Discount Given** – Total discount amount offered to customers.
* **On-Time Delivery Rate** – Percentage of orders delivered within the expected delivery time.
